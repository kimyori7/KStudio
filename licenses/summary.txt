KStudio — Third-Party Components (summary)

own license; this section lists the projects, their licenses, and links to the
upstream sources. The full texts of the GPL v3 and LGPL v3, and the FFmpeg
corresponding-source provision, follow this summary in this same file.

For the licenses of KStudio's own source code, see LICENSE.

- Exact build: ffmpeg 2026-04-19-git-de18feb0f0-essentials_build (gyan.dev).
- Corresponding Source (GPL v3 sec.6) and full build provenance: see the
